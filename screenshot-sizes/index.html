App Store & Google Play screenshot sizes (November 2026)

Short answer: for the App Store you need one iPhone size (1179 × 2556), plus 2064 × 2752 if your app runs on iPad. Google Play wants at least 4 portrait shots at 1080 × 1920 or larger. Everything else is optional.

Apple App Store

Formats: JPEG or PNG, no transparency (alpha), 1 to 10 screenshots per device size. Source: App Store Connect "Screenshot specifications".

The simple recipe

  1. Design once at 1290 × 2796 (iPhone large display, 9:19.5).
  2. Export that and 1179 × 2556. It's the same aspect ratio, so one layout scales cleanly.
  3. If you support iPad, export 2064 × 2752.
  4. For Google Play, export 1080 × 1920 (4 or more) plus the 1024 × 500 feature graphic.

Captions that work

The first screenshot is read in under two seconds. Lead with the outcome, not the feature, and keep it to 3 to 7 words: "Track every habit in seconds", "Never lose a receipt again", "See every subscription at a glance".

FAQ

What size are App Store screenshots in 2026?

The one required iPhone size is 1179 x 2556 (or 1206 x 2622) for iPhones with Dynamic Island. If your app runs on iPad, you also need 2064 x 2752 (or 2048 x 2732). Other sizes are optional and scaled from the next size up.

Do I still need 6.5-inch or 6.7-inch screenshots?

Only if you don't supply the Dynamic Island sizes. 1284 x 2778 / 1242 x 2688 (Face ID large display) are required only when Dynamic Island large-display screenshots are missing. 1290 x 2796 is optional but a good master size to design at.

What size are Google Play screenshots?

Between 320 and 3840 px per side, with the long side at most twice the short side. To be eligible for Play's recommendation placements, upload at least 4 at 1080 px or larger, for example 1080 x 1920 portrait.

Can screenshots have a transparent background?

No. Both stores reject alpha channels in screenshots. Export JPEG or a PNG without transparency.
